## The joke

> This meeting could have been an email, but then nobody would have heard me say 'great question' for 40 minutes.

1 meeting, 40 minutes, 0 emails sent: the setup is the universal office complaint — the meeting could have been an email — then the joke turns on the one phrase everyone performs in meetings and nobody means, and the number makes it true, because the meeting was forty minutes long and the phrase was the only thing in it.
